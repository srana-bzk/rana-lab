Study Guide: Software and Operating Systems

Intro to Computer Science · Quiz: Thursday, October 22

The quiz covers everything on this study guide, and nothing else. It has 4 short real-life stories, 50 points total, plus a 10 point bonus. Read the story, find the clue, and pick the best answer. No notes.

1. Hardware vs. Software

Hardware is the parts you can touch, like RAM, a mouse, or a monitor. Software is the instructions that make hardware do stuff, like Chrome, Windows, or Minecraft.

Quick test: can you touch it? Then it's hardware. Hardware is what the computer is. Software is what it does.

2. The Two Teams of Software

System softwareApplication software (apps)
What it doesRuns and manages the computer, in the background.Helps you do a specific task.
Ask yourselfDoes it serve the computer?Does it serve me?
ExamplesOperating systems, drivers, and utilities.Chrome, YouTube, Minecraft, Google Docs, Spotify, and Calculator.
School matchThe principal and office staff.Your classes, like art, PE, and CS.

The system software crew

What it doesExamplesSchool match
Operating systemThe computer's manager. It connects you, your apps, and the hardware.Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, and Linux.The principal.
Device driverThe translator. It helps the operating system talk to one piece of hardware.A printer driver, keyboard driver, or graphics card driver.An interpreter.
UtilityThe cleanup and security team. It keeps the computer healthy.Antivirus, Disk Cleanup, and backup tools.Custodians and security.

Remember: a new printer or device won't work? Check the driver. Apps need an operating system to run. No operating system means nothing works.

3. The Operating System's 4 Jobs

You ask. The operating system coordinates. The hardware does it.

JobWhat the OS doesSchool matchReal-life example
1. Manage hardwareDecides who gets to use the CPU, RAM, storage, printer, and screen, and shares them fairly.Assigning rooms and equipment.Sending your essay to the printer.
2. User interfaceGives you a way to talk to the computer. A GUI means clicking icons and menus. A CLI means typing commands.The front desk.Clicking the Chrome icon.
3. Manage filesCreates, saves, names, moves, finds, and protects your files and folders.The records office.Saving your essay in a folder, or renaming a photo.
4. Manage programsLoads apps into RAM and shares CPU time so many apps can run at once.The bell schedule.Spotify playing while you play a game.

What happens when you open an app?

  1. You click it. That is the user interface.
  2. The operating system finds the program. That is managing files.
  3. The operating system loads it into RAM. That is managing programs.
  4. The CPU runs it. That is managing hardware.
  5. You see it on the screen. That is the user interface again.

Four jobs, one click, and less than one second.

4. Common Operating Systems

Every operating system does the same 4 jobs, just on different devices.

Operating systemDevice
WindowsMost PCs and laptops, like Dell, HP, and Lenovo.
macOSMac computers, like a MacBook or iMac.
iOSiPhones.
AndroidSamsung phones and many others.

Practice Questions

Try these on your own, then check your answers with Mr. Rana. They look just like the quiz.

1. Write H for hardware or S for software: Instagram, RAM, Windows 11, a mouse, and a printer driver.

2. Leo plugs a brand new webcam into his laptop. The laptop says device not recognized. What is most likely missing?

  • A. More RAM.
  • B. The webcam's driver.
  • C. A new app.
  • D. A bigger screen.

3. Which of these are application software? Minecraft, macOS, Google Docs, antivirus, YouTube, and a printer driver.

4. Kayla is using her laptop. Which operating system job is each action? She taps the Spotify icon. She renames a photo and moves it into a folder. Spotify keeps playing while she plays Roblox. Her essay is sent to the printer.

5. Name the operating system for each device: an iPhone, a Samsung phone, a MacBook, and a Dell laptop.

6. Your friend says, "I don't need an operating system. I only use apps." Is your friend right? Explain why or why not.

7. You tap the YouTube app. Put these steps in order. The operating system loads YouTube into RAM. You tap the YouTube icon. The CPU runs YouTube's instructions. The operating system finds the YouTube program in storage. You see YouTube on your screen.
